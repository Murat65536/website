I often face a dilemma as a student with high asperations where I just don't know what I should be doing at the moment, or whether what I'm currently doing is inefficient or a waste of time. I believe that as I age, the road ahead of me gets less and less malliable, which puts a lot of pressure on me to make the most of the years I have left.
My issues is that I don't know how to act. Should I study schoolwork? Should I code? Should I research opportunities? Should I go all-in with AI or not use it at all? Should I network?

I have no answer to any of this. Nothing to ground myself on, and even if I do pick something to go with, I have no way of knowing whether or not I'm wasting my time, or whether my determination of that is even right. I'm flying blind.
Many have the lamp of experience to guide their feet, but I have nothing.

On top of all of this, I have emotions, states of mind, and past events that all may supplement or detriment my current and future situation. This is why, for example, relying on Anki as a crutch has not completely worked out for me for learning things. I simply get bored. While studying, I might get sleepy or distracted too. It's all really terrible, and I feel inefficient, especially when I see AI systems excell in minutes on tasks that took me months. It somewhat makes me think that I should give this large question to AI. Throw everything about me to AI and say "hey, how do I live from now on?" I really do wonder how it'll go about answering such a difficult question. I've tried to implement it somewhat through having it automatically scan my school assignments and create TaskWarrior entries but I feel like it's not enough, because that tells me "yes, I COULD be doing that, and I will eventually have to, but that's one of the many things I could be doing, and it's not immedietly due, so... what if I procrastinate or do something less productive?" Maybe the solution is time blocking by the hour, but that risks demotivation from the system if I start failing to meet the deadlines. Regardless, it might be the method.

I'll probably post again once I've tried it and seen if it works.
